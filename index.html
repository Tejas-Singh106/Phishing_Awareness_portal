KNOW THE THREAT

What is phishing?

Phishing is a social-engineering attack where criminals imitate trusted websites, messages, or services to trick people into revealing information or taking unsafe actions.

01

Look-alike domains

Attackers may use misspellings, extra words, unusual subdomains, or misleading domain names.

02

Urgent messages

Pressure such as “verify now” or “account suspended” is often used to rush a decision.

03

Fake login pages

A convincing copy of a real service may be used to capture usernames and passwords.

STAY SAFE

Quick safety checklist

✓ Check the domain carefully before signing in.
✓ Avoid clicking unexpected links in messages.
✓ Use bookmarks for important services.
✓ Enable multi-factor authentication.
✓ Never share OTPs or passwords through links.
✓ When unsure, open the official website yourself.
